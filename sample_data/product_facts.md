# Verdant Product Fact Sheet 2026

## Everyday Bottle

The Verdant Everyday Bottle is made with 70% recycled stainless steel and is fully recyclable at end of life. It keeps drinks cold for 24 hours and hot for 12 hours. It is covered by a 5 year warranty.

## Packaging

All Verdant orders ship in kraft paper packaging made from 100% recycled paper that is recyclable in standard kerbside collection. Verdant packaging contains no plastic fillers.

## Terra Cleaning Concentrate

Terra cleaning concentrate is plant-based and biodegradable within 28 days under OECD 301B testing. The bottle is made from 50% recycled plastic. Terra is not certified organic.

## Solar Lantern

The Verdant Solar Lantern runs for 20 hours on a full charge and charges in 6 hours of direct sunlight. It is water resistant to IPX4.

## Carbon & Sustainability Status

Verdant offsets the shipping emissions of every order. Verdant as a company is not certified carbon neutral and does not currently claim zero waste operations. Verdant does not claim to be 100% eco-friendly; individual product facts above are the only approved environmental claims.

## Pricing & Offers

The Everyday Bottle retails at 24 dollars. The member loyalty programme gives 10% off every third order. Free shipping applies to orders above 40 dollars.
