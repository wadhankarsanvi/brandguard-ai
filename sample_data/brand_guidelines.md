# Verdant Brand Guidelines v4.2

## 1. Voice & Tone

Verdant sounds calm, warm and confident. We explain, we never shout. Use at most one exclamation mark per 100 words and avoid writing words in ALL CAPS. Avoid hype words such as revolutionary, game-changing, mind-blowing, unbeatable or best-ever; let facts do the persuading. Keep sentences short and clear, under 25 words where possible.

## 2. Terminology

Our name is written "Verdant" with a capital V. Never write VerdantCo, Verdant Co or verdant in lower case when referring to the brand. We call buyers "members", never "customers". We describe our prices as "affordable" or "fairly priced", never "cheap". Address readers as "everyone" or "friends", never "guys". Use "use" instead of "utilize".

## 3. Claims & Substantiation

Every environmental, performance or comparative claim must be backed by an approved source in the Product Fact Sheet. Do not use absolute claims such as 100%, guaranteed, always, number one, or best unless the fact sheet states exactly that. Words like eco-friendly, carbon neutral, zero waste, biodegradable, compostable and recyclable are regulated environmental claims. If the fact sheet does not support a claim, soften it or remove it. Numbers must match the fact sheet exactly.

## 4. Legal Disclaimers

Any promotion, discount, free trial, money-back or guarantee offer must include the line "Terms and conditions apply." Health, financial or performance outcomes must add "Results may vary." Legal must review every piece that contains an environmental claim before it is published.

## 5. Visual Identity

The approved palette is Verdant Green #2E7D32, Deep Forest #1B3A2D, Sunrise Amber #F5A623 and Cloud White #F7F9F7. Neutral greys and black are allowed for text. Dominant colours outside this palette are not permitted on campaign assets. The Verdant logo needs clear space equal to one quarter of the logo height on all sides. Images must be at least 800 pixels wide for web use.

## 6. Privacy

Never publish personal data: personal email addresses, phone numbers, payment card numbers or customer names. Use the shared inbox hello@verdant.example for contact details. Unreleased product information must not be shared with external AI tools without masking.

## 7. Accessibility

All content must meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Every image needs meaningful alt text that describes what it conveys; never start alt text with "image of" or use a filename. Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text). Link text must describe the destination: never use "click here" or "read more".
